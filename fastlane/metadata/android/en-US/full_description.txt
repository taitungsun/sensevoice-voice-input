Offline voice input for Android. Speak Chinese or English; Chinese comes out in Traditional characters. Everything runs on the phone; the network is used only once, to download the model.

Designed for low-end keypad phones such as the Qin F21 Pro, with seniors and anyone who finds typing hard in mind. Tested on the Qin F21 Pro; it should also work on other arm64 phones with Android 8.0 or later. The app's screens are in Traditional Chinese.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Works as Android's system voice input, e.g. from the voice key in TT9</li>
<li>Spoken punctuation: say "comma", "period", "new line" and so on</li>
<li>No automatic full stop at the end of a sentence</li>
<li>Large text, full keypad navigation, TalkBack support</li>
<li>No ads, no tracking; the app collects and sends nothing</li>
</ul>

<b>Before first use</b>
<ul>
<li>In the app, download SenseVoice Small (239 MB)</li>
<li>Settings → Languages &amp; input → Voice input → choose this app</li>
</ul>

The network is used only to download the speech model from HuggingFace, which sees that one download like any website; you can turn it off afterwards. SenseVoice Small is by FunAudioLLM (Alibaba Group) under the FunASR Model License.
